## Method notes

- A break-out is a post at 3x or more the median of its author's comparable earlier posts (the 20 before it, videos with videos), over at least 8 of them; 1.5x is above usual. The post itself is left out of its own base.
- Who is read: X from 10,000 views; TikTok from 10,000 views; Instagram from 300 likes (the windowed reel search has no views); YouTube from 10,000 views; Reddit from 200 score; LinkedIn from 50 likes.
- On Reddit a post's score is compared with its community's usual score.
- Posts about data APIs, scraping services or data vendors are left out, ours included (2 this run).
- Searches use each platform's own week where a date range is refused; 0 rows outside 20/11/2026 to 27/11/2026 were dropped, and 1 replies and reposts.
- Accounts under 10,000 followers are described, not named. A post that may be boosted is flagged and not counted as a break-out.
- Baseline credits per platform: X 12, TikTok 10, Instagram 5, YouTube 8, Reddit 4, LinkedIn 10.
- Findings are labelled seen, seen, but self-reported, inferred, unknown; a count that could not be read is shown as unknown (unknown, not zero).
